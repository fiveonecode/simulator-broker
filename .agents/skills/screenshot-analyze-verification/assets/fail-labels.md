# Standard Failure Labels

Use these exact labels when the failure fits.
Prefer one primary label per defect.

- `NOT VERIFIED: wrong app/page/sim capture`
- `NOT VERIFIED: stale build or stale screenshot evidence`
- `NOT VERIFIED: missing or cropped required element`
- `NOT VERIFIED: hidden-element suppression leak`
- `NOT VERIFIED: insufficient text contrast or legibility`
- `NOT VERIFIED: overlap, clipping, or spacing defect`
- `NOT VERIFIED: pointer or tail detached/mis-targeted`
- `NOT VERIFIED: incomplete simulator or theme coverage`
- `NOT VERIFIED: screenshot does not prove requested interaction state`
